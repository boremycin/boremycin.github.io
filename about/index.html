Yiming Zhang

M.S. Student · Fudan University

Email GitHub Google Scholar CV (PDF)

I am a master's student in Information and Communication Engineering at Fudan University, advised by Prof. Feng Wang. My research focuses on adversarial robustness and security in machine learning, with a particular emphasis on synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery and its physics-driven image formation process.

Research Interests

  • Adversarial attacks against SAR classification and object detection models.
  • Adversarial training and robust learning for SAR semantic segmentation.
  • Security, robustness, and interpretability of large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs).
